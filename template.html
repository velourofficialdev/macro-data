Options positioning

From Cboe's free delayed option chains after the US close: open interest (settled, start of session) and closing prices. Gamma exposure assumes dealers hold the opposite side of customers who are long calls and long puts, a common simplification; it maps where hedging flows may cluster, not where price must go. S&P levels come from SPX options and are shifted to ES by the closing basis; gold levels come from GLD options scaled to the COMEX gold future. Not a live trade tape.

FX confluence

Daily timeframe. Score 0-100 starts at 50 and adds four signals: day-on-day momentum (±12.5), RSI(14) (up to ±25), price vs 50-day EMA (±15) and 200-day Z-score (up to ±20); 60+ bullish, 40 or less bearish. Support and resistance: the 120-day high and low (strong) and levels 40% of the way from price to each (moderate). Same formula as the original FX Macro Confluence engine. Pairs from the ECB's official euro reference rates (daily 14:15 CET fixing, crossed through the euro; Yahoo then FRED as fallbacks); XAUUSD uses COMEX gold futures. Latest close __FXUPD__.
